## Known research gaps
- No authoritative public source found for a standard *packaging error rate* formula or for
  *paperwork discrepancy* controls — v1 definitions are our assumptions.
- AS9100/ISO 9001 clause text not reviewed (paywalled); only public summaries used.
- Dock-to-stock benchmarks conflict across sources; WERC report not reviewed.
- Specific numeric dashboard rules (≤ 7 visuals) are from secondary sources, not verified against
  Stephen Few's original books.
